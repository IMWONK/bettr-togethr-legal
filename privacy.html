3. Information you give us

Account information. Your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored only as a cryptographic hash. We never see or store your password itself.

Profile information. First name, last name, username, and optionally a profile photo and a short bio.

Your Why, and who you are becoming. The statement you write during onboarding and any later version of it. We never overwrite a previous Why; the history is retained so the app can show you how your reason has changed over time.

Growth areas and goals. The areas you select and any goals you create, including titles, descriptions, target values and dates.

Habits and habit completions. The habits you create and the days you mark them complete.

Workouts. Workouts, exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, durations and distances. Weight is stored in kilograms, distance in meters and duration in seconds regardless of the units you have chosen to see.

Journal content. Gratitude entries and reflections, including the mood rating you attach to a reflection.

Wins. The wins you record, and whether you chose to keep each one private or share it with the community.

Community content. Posts, comments, and celebrations you send. Friend requests you send and accept. Users you block. Reports you submit about content or other users, including the reason and any detail you write.

Settings. Your timezone, unit preferences, week start day, default content visibility, notification type preferences and quiet hours.

3a. Information other people create about you

Two kinds of record are created about you by someone other than you. You should know they exist.

Reports about your content. If another user reports something you posted, a record is created that identifies the reported content, the reason chosen, and any detail the reporter wrote. That record is about you even though you did not create it, and it is retained after review so that a pattern of behaviour remains visible rather than resetting each time.

You are not notified when you are reported, and you are not shown who reported you. Both are deliberate: telling someone who reported them is how retaliation starts.

Moderation records. If we hide, restore, or otherwise act on your content, or suspend your account, a record of that action is created, including what was acted on, what action was taken, and why. This is an audit trail. It is retained so that moderation decisions can be reviewed and reversed, and so that repeated violations are visible rather than forgotten.

You can request a copy of the moderation records concerning you by emailing support@bettrtogethr.net. Reports about your content are not included in that copy where doing so would reveal who reported you.

4. Information collected automatically

Push notification tokens. If you allow push notifications, the app registers a device token so notifications can reach that device. A token identifies a device installation, not you personally. Tokens for devices you have signed out of are deactivated, not deleted, so that the record of which devices were registered remains accurate.

Activity events. The app records an append-only log of significant occurrences in your account — a habit completed, a workout finished, a win recorded, a friendship accepted. This log is what drives milestones, your Why Moments and your progress statistics. It is tied to your account and is not shared with anyone.

Crash and error reports. When the app encounters an error, a report is sent to our error-monitoring provider so the problem can be fixed. These reports are deliberately scrubbed before they are sent: your Why text, gratitude entries, reflections, win text, post bodies and comment bodies are removed. What remains is the error itself, the type of operation that failed, and identifiers.

What we do not collect. We do not collect your precise or approximate location. We do not collect your contacts, photos library, microphone, camera roll beyond a photo you deliberately choose as an avatar, health data from Apple Health or any other health platform, advertising identifiers, or browsing activity outside this app.

5. How we use your information

We use the information above only to:

  • Create and maintain your account and sign you in.
  • Show you your own content, statistics and progress.
  • Show content you chose to share with the community to the audience you chose.
  • Deliver notifications you have turned on, at times you have not marked quiet.
  • Filter content for prohibited material before it is published, and act on reports of content that violates our Terms.
  • Diagnose and fix errors and crashes.
  • Respond when you contact us.

We do not build advertising or marketing profiles. We do not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

7. Where your information is stored

Your information is stored on servers in the United States. If you use the app from outside the United States, you are sending your information to the United States, where privacy law differs from the law where you live.

8. Security

Access to your data is enforced at the database level, so a request for another user's private content is refused by the database itself rather than only by the app. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our hosting provider.

Some information is cached on your device so the app works offline. Your Why text, gratitude entries, reflections and wins are deliberately excluded from that cache, because device storage is not encrypted in a way we control.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise. If we become aware of a breach affecting your information, we will notify you at the email address on your account and comply with applicable breach notification law.

9. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists.

Content you delete inside the app — a win, a gratitude entry, a reflection, a post, a comment — is immediately hidden from every view and is permanently erased on the schedule described below.

When you delete your account, the account enters a 30-day window during which it can be recovered if you change your mind or the deletion was not intended. After that window, all of your information is permanently erased from our systems, including your profile, every entry you wrote, every workout, every post and comment, your event history and your notification tokens.

Two things survive that erasure, both deliberately:

  • Reports you submitted about other users' content are retained without your identity attached, because a safety record that disappears when the reporter leaves is not a safety record.
  • Backups are retained on a rolling basis by our hosting provider for a short period and expire on their own schedule. We do not restore deleted accounts from backups.

10. Your rights

Regardless of where you live, you can:

  • See and export everything. Settings → Account → Export data produces a complete copy of the information tied to your account.
  • Correct it. Profile, Why, settings and most content can be edited in the app. Email support@bettrtogethr.net for anything you cannot reach.
  • Delete it. Settings → Account → Delete account, as described above.

If you live in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, Oregon, Montana, or another state with a comprehensive consumer privacy law, you also have the right to confirm whether we process your information, to obtain a portable copy, to correct inaccuracies, to request deletion, and to be free from discrimination for exercising any of these rights. Because we do not sell personal information, do not share it for targeted advertising, and do not profile users for decisions with legal effects, there is no opt-out to exercise for those activities — they do not happen.

To make a request, email support@bettrtogethr.net or write to the address in section 1. We will respond within 45 days. We may ask you to confirm you control the account's email address before acting, because we have no other way to know a request is really yours. You may use an authorized agent; we will ask for proof of their authority.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, our lawful basis for processing is the performance of our contract with you (operating the app), your consent (push notifications), and our legitimate interests (keeping the service secure and functioning). You have the right to object to processing, to restrict it, and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.
